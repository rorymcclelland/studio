# 0017 Redraw with the current kit, and ads for every audience

**Date:** 2 November 2026. **Status:** accepted (built in Sprint 7: F-26 and F-27).

**Context.** Decision 0014 froze the kit into each ad (a database guard stops `kit` changing) and composed ads for the brief's first audience only. F-26 asks for a free way to bring ads up to date after a logo, palette or font change; F-27 asks for ads for every audience, so the launch pack covers the whole brief.

**Decision.**
- **A redraw is a new variant, not an edit.** The guard on `kit` stays. Redraw with current kit makes a new row with origin `redrawn` (migration `20261102000001_redrawn_origin.sql` widens the origin check), `based_on` the old ad, the same picture, words and layout, the brand's kit as it is now and the old ad's pick; the old ad is discarded, in one transaction (discard first, so the one-current-variant-per-picture-and-words index holds). It is drawn in code, so it costs nothing and has no run; the person's click is the approval (rule 3), as for New picture.
- **"Made with an older kit"** means the ad's stored kit differs from `kitSnapshot()` of its brand now. They are compared as canonical JSON (keys sorted), because jsonb reorders keys and the stored kit would otherwise never equal a freshly built one.
- **Same layout only.** A redraw is drawn in the ad's own layout. If the renderer refuses (for example a palette with no colours, so no text reaches AA contrast), the redraw is refused with the renderer's reason and the old ad is kept; it does not silently move to another layout. An ad with a New picture being drawn is not redrawn (the picture's run would then find its ad discarded).
- **Redraw all N older ads** redraws each older current ad of the signed-off version the same way, saves those that drew in one transaction, and lists the ones kept on the older kit with their reasons; if none could be redrawn it is refused with the reasons.
- **Undo is Restore.** Restoring an ad that a redraw replaced discards the redrawn ad and brings the old one back with the redrawn ad's pick. Only the direct redraw is undone this way: after two redraws in a row, the middle one is restored first.
- **Every audience gets ads.** Make creative composes the four pictures with every audience's paid-social and display sets (pictures shared), in the brief's audience order. The gallery groups ads by audience under a heading per audience (the set's `segment_title`) when there are several, and stays as it was when there is one, including a brief that names none ("Everyone the brief is for"). Ads are numbered through the whole gallery so each ad's name stays unique. The Make creative estimate is unchanged: the pictures are shared, and the Copy agent already wrote every audience's words in one run.
- **Ad words without ads** (every ad on them discarded, or a campaign whose ads were made before F-27, which has ads for its first audience only) are listed beside the gallery with Edit, New words and Discard, replacing the old "Ad words for the other audiences" list.

**Consequences.** A campaign made before this sprint keeps ads for its first audience only; its other audiences' words show under "Ad words without ads", and Make creative stays refused while the version has ads. Composing those audiences' ads without new pictures would be a small follow-up (composition already skips pairs that exist). A redrawn ad moves to the end of its picture's ads in the gallery, as replacements do. Make creative draws twice as many ads for two audiences (about 0.2 to 0.3 s each), so 48 ads for two audiences with display.
