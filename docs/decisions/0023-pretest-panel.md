# 0023 The pre-test panel: synthetic personas score picked ads, with scores only

**Date:** 3 November 2026. **Status:** accepted (built in Sprint 8: C8-4, with Rory's answer Q6).

**Context.** Before any spend, a marketer wants to see which picked ads are likely to land with which audience, and why. The brief's personas (S4-2, `src/lib/agents/persona.ts`) already score the brief; nothing scored ads. A model asked to "pre-test" an ad will happily give a click rate, a percentage of people who would buy, or a quote the ad never said, and any of those would be an invented metric (rule 1). Forecasts belong to the simulator (Sprint 9). Rule 3 says a person starts anything that costs; rule 5 that every call goes through `generate()` and the cap; rule 7 that every table has `workspace_id` and RLS.

**Decision.**
- **The Pretest agent** (`src/lib/agents/pretest.ts`, Creative squad) on **Haiku with vision**, one call per ad: the ad drawn as its 1080 square PNG is the picture, and the prompt holds the brand's voice, the channel, the ad's audience, the ad's words and legal lines (as JSON data lines after the task marker) and the personas. Haiku because the job is short judgement on one picture and short sentences, and it costs about **0.05p an ad** (2,800 input tokens with the picture, 800 output, at Haiku's price); Sonnet would be about twenty times that for a panel that is labelled synthetic anyway.
- **The panel is the brief's personas**: those of the latest persona review for the signed-off version (`persona_reviews`), up to four. No new personas are written. If the signed-off version has no review yet (it is started at sign-off), the button says so and nothing runs.
- **Four scales, 1 to 5, whole numbers**: stops the scroll, clear, believable, on brand. Each has one sentence of reason and a quote, copied exactly from the ad's words.
- **Checks in code, with one repair** (`checkPretest`, as decision 0009): every persona given scores once and only once; a reason's own words (the ad's quoted words taken out, so an ad that says "Save 5%" can be repeated) hold no percentage, no digit, no quantity in words (`quantityInWords`) and no prediction (click rates, conversions, forecasts, "likely to buy", "will click" and the like); each quote is in the ad's headline, body, button or legal lines, ignoring case, curly quotes, soft hyphens and spacing, and is at least two words or a whole field. Any problem gets the one repair `runModelStep` gives; a reply that still fails is refused and the ad is listed in the run as not scored, with the reason.
- **Only on a click, picked ads only, with the cost shown** (Q6). **Pre-test picked ads** sits beside the Languages bar with "about 0.05p an ad, 0.2p in all" on it (`pretestEstimate`, `src/lib/creative/costs.ts`). It scores the picked ads of the signed-off version that have no current scores; it is refused with the reason for a draft brief, nothing picked, no panel, every picked ad already scored, or while a pre-test runs for the campaign. The busy check and the run's insert are one transaction under an advisory lock keyed by the campaign (`takeRunLock`, `src/lib/agents/runs.ts`), so two clicks at once start one paid run (review fix 4; Check pictures and Check an ad do the same, keyed by the campaign and by the upload). It is never run automatically, and nothing is ranked, unpicked or changed: the scores sit beside the ad.
- **Scores are kept with the ad** in **`pretest_scores`** (`db/migrations/20261103000003_pretest_scores.sql`): `workspace_id`, the campaign, the ad, its picture and words, its **key** (the ad's ETag: picture hash, copy set, layout, kit and renderer version), the persona review's run, the run and step, the panel (each persona's name, segment, average and scores), the average and whether the mock model wrote it. Row-level security as every table (select and insert for members, `created_by` the user), parents referenced by `(id, workspace_id)`, a trigger that the picture and words are the ad's own, and append-only (update, delete, truncate revoked; anon has nothing).
- **Out of date, never carried over.** A redraw, new words or a new picture make a new ad based on the old one (decisions 0014 and 0017), and a layout switch changes the key. The gallery looks for scores on the ad and then on the ads it was made from: the ad's own scores with an unchanged key, from the panel that is still the brief's, are current; anything else shows **"Out of date: pre-test again"** and no scores, and the next Pre-test picked ads scores it again. Running the persona review again gives the brief version a new panel, so every ad's scores from the old panel go out of date too (review nit 10).
- **Labels (rule 1).** Every score shows **"Synthetic panel, not real customers"**, with "(simulated scores)" when the mock model wrote them. The words never call them results, and the bar says they are "scores, not results or predictions". The run's summary starts with the label.
- **The gallery**: each pre-tested ad shows "Panel average 3.8 out of 5" and the label, and a disclosure with each persona's name, segment, average, and the four scales, each with its score, reason and quote.
- **The launch pack's manifest** (`manifest.json`) gains `pack.labels.syntheticPanel` and `pack.pretest`: the label, a note that the scores are not results or predictions, and for each picked ad its status (current, "Out of date: pre-test again", or not pre-tested) and, when current, the average and each persona's scores and reasons; each file entry carries its ad's average with the label. These are in their own functions in `pack.ts` (`panelScores`, `panelAverageFor`). The CSV and README are unchanged.

**Consequences.** The quote check is literal, so a model that paraphrases the ad is repaired, and a reason that mentions "half the words" would be refused as a quantity; both cost one more call at most (about 0.1p). The panel is only as good as the persona review it comes from, and it inherits that review's version: a brief signed off again needs its own review before its ads can be pre-tested. Scores for an earlier version of an ad are kept (append-only) and are reachable through the ad's history, but never shown as its scores. The real model's reasons are a hand check on production (C8-4 #5, the Sprint 8 test pack), as CI runs the mock model. Turning scores into forecasts, and ranking ads for launch, are out of scope (the simulator, Sprint 9).
