# 0022 Compliance checks: rules in code over laid-out boxes, decisions tied to a finding's hash

**Date:** 3 November 2026. **Status:** accepted (built in Sprint 8: C8-1, with Rory's answers Q1, Q2, Q3, Q5 and Q7).

**Context.** Until Sprint 8 the renderer either drew an ad or refused it (decision 0012), so nothing said that an ad which drew was close to a limit, or broke a rule no code checked. A marketer needs every picked ad, in every size (0018) and language (0019), checked against the brand's rules and the channel's, with pass, review or fail, the reason and a fix, and the launch pack (0020) must hold only what passes or what a person accepted. Rule 1 says the thresholds are Studio's own; rule 3 that people approve what spends, publishes or replaces creative; rule 4 that decisions name who and when; rule 7 that every table carries `workspace_id` with row-level security.

**Decision.**
- **Boxes from the plan.** `planAd` (`render.ts`) now also returns where it lays each part out (`boxes.ts`): one box per drawn line of the headline, body, button words and legal lines (the measured width by the line height), the button, the logo's mark and plate, the picture and the "Simulated image" pill. They follow the flex layouts in `layouts.tsx` step by step and are computed beside the drawing, never feeding it: the square's golden pixel hashes (C7-1.6) are unchanged. `fitAd` returns them with the recorded contrast and the legal lines drawn, as `plan`, so `fitInSizes` and the fit cache (`fit-cache.ts`) carry them without drawing.
- **Rules in code, no model, no cost** (`src/lib/compliance/rules.ts`, pure functions with unit tests). Thresholds in one file, `src/config/compliance.ts` (Q1), stated as Studio's own defaults, not any platform's policy:

  | Rule | Pass | Review | Fail |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Text contrast | words and button words 4.5:1, button edge 3:1 | under 10% above a threshold (4.5 to 4.95:1 for words), with a palette colour that would do better | below a threshold (only an uploaded ad, C8-3) |
  | Legal lines present | every kit line for the language drawn | none | a line missing, or a language without lines while English has some |
  | Banned terms | none of the language's or English terms in the words | none | one found, quoted |
  | Logo clear space | 4% of the shorter side or more, to every edge and word | 2% to 4% | under 2%, or touching a word |
  | Text density | words cover 25% or less | 25% to 40% | over 40% |
  | 9:16 platform zones | no words, button or logo in the top 14% or bottom 20% | within 4% of a band | inside a band |
  | Don't rule | none | each kit don't rule, once per ad | none (a person may mark it not acceptable) |
  | Simulated label | drawn on a simulated picture | none | can't happen |

  Text density is **not applied to banners** (Q2), shown as "Not applied to banners: their words naturally fill them"; the logo rule is not applied to a brand with no logo. The platform-zone rule is listed only for the 9:16 story (Q7). The "Simulated image" pill is Studio's label, not the ad's words, so it counts for none of the geometric rules. Each fix is a suggestion only; nothing changes an ad.
- **Every layout keeps clear of a story's bands** (Rory, while building). Every 9:16 layout first put words in the bottom 20%. Each now keeps its words, logo and legal lines below the top band and above the bottom one, with their 4% margin and a pixel more (`storyClearance` in `layouts.tsx`, from the config): Full bleed raises its words (its panel grows by the same amount, so the words keep their room) and lowers its logo; Split lowers its logo and gives its picture band what the bottom takes; Card insets its words. The picture holds no words and may run under the bands. Only the 9:16 drawings change; `SIZES_VERSION` is 3. Measured with the mock Copy agent's words and their mock translations for both demo brands (390 layouts and sizes drawn), every drawn ad passes the zones, the logo rule, text density and contrast; the fits that refuse (Split with the longer translations, the leaderboard's legal lines or button) are the same as before. The rule's "Use the Card layout" fix now applies only to an ad laid out another way (an uploaded one, C8-3).
- **Banner logos at 4%** (Rory, while building). The medium rectangle and half page place their corner logo at the logo rule's clear space (4% of the shorter side, and a pixel more: 11 px and 13 px) from both edges, so they pass without review. Only those two drawings change; the leaderboard's logo sits in its row as before.
- **Findings on request, from the kit as it is now.** `src/lib/compliance/compute.ts` reads the ads, the brand's current kit (legal lines, banned terms, don't rules, per-language lines and terms) and the decisions through `asUser`, lays each ad out with `fitsFor` (the same cached fits the gallery's thumbnails and the launch pack use, so one fit serves all three) and runs the rules. Findings are not stored: they can be worked out again, and a banned term added to the kit shows at once. The rules themselves take microseconds, so only the fits are cached.
- **Decisions** are rows in `compliance_decisions` (migration `20261103000001`): `workspace_id`, the ad by `(variant_id, workspace_id)`, `lang` and `size` (both null for a don't rule), the rule's key, `finding_hash`, `accept` or `reject`, a note of at most 500 characters, and `decided_by`, `decided_by_name` and `decided_at`, which a trigger sets from the session whatever the insert says (rule 4). The table is append-only (select and insert policies; update, delete, truncate revoked), so the latest decision counts and the history stays. A finding's hash covers what it says and the drawing it is about (the ad's ETag in that size and language, which holds the picture, words, layout and frozen kit), so a decision applies only while the finding is unchanged: a redraw, new words, a layout switch or an edited rule makes it Review again. A don't rule is about the whole ad, so its hash covers the English ad's ETag and the ad's current translations (each language and its copy set; review fix 5): a translation added after a person accepted the rule makes it Review again, because they accepted the ad in the languages it had then, and Accept for every picked ad covers the new item in one click. The picture's findings (C8-2) are about the picture alone, which a translation doesn't change, so they keep the English ad's ETag. Studio has no display names, so the name recorded is the person's email.
- **Inline, not in the approvals inbox** (Q3). A review item is decided on the ad's Compliance view (`/campaigns/<id>/ads/<ad>/compliance`, beside Sizes) with Accept or Not acceptable and an optional note. It changes what a download holds, not what is published or spent. A decision is refused when the finding has changed since the page was drawn, or is not a review item. Not acceptable counts as a fail. **Accept for every picked ad** (Rory, while building): on the campaign, under the compliance summary, each of the brand's don't rules can be accepted for every picked ad of the signed-off version at once. Each ad still gets its own decision row, with the person's name and the time and tied to its own finding hash, exactly as if accepted on its Compliance view; ads already decided on the rule, including any a person marked not acceptable, are left as they are.
- **The gallery** shows a pill per ad (Passes, N to review, Fails; open review items counted over the ad and every drawn file) and a summary line over the picked ads' drawn files ("16 ads pass, 3 to review, 1 fails"). Both stream in on their own Suspense boundaries, like the launch pack.
- **The launch pack** (Q5) leaves out every file with a fail ("Fails: banned term 'guaranteed' in German.") or an unaccepted review ("Awaiting review: don't rule \"...\"."), listed with the reason in the gallery and both manifests; one reason for every size of a language is listed once, as "every size". Each file in `manifest.json` gains `compliance`: its outcome (`passes`, or `accepted` when a person accepted a review item) and every rule with its status, reason, and who accepted it, when and with what note. `manifest.csv` gains `compliance` ("Passes, 3 review items accepted") and `reviewed_by`; the README gains one line on the rules. A pack in which nothing passes is refused with the reason (a 409 at the address), never an empty zip. The findings are worked out before the pack reads its words and layouts, in another transaction, so each file's findings carry the copy set and layout they were checked for, and a file whose words or layout the pack would draw differently (a translation or a layout switch in between) is held back with "The ad's words or layout changed while the pack was being planned ... Build the pack again", never packed under another drawing's checks (review fix 3). A rule from the picture check records who checked it (`checkedBy`, "Checked by AI"), and the AI's "no" is recorded as `"no issues found by AI"`, never as `"pass"` (review fix 2); the Compliance view shows it the same way for the face rule and the don't rules (nit 7).

**Consequences.** Every ad waits for a person until its brand's don't rules are accepted, once per ad; that is the point (no code can read a don't rule), and Accept for every picked ad makes it one click per rule before a pack. Text density for the demo words is 6% to 19%, well inside 25%. The boxes are accurate to a few pixels a line (Satori's shaping, 0012), so a measurement right at a threshold could read either side of it; the layouts keep a pixel's margin for that. A 9:16 Full bleed's panel now covers more of its picture (about 400 px), and a 9:16 Split's picture is 14 px smaller (1066 px). The C7-3 fixtures are as they were before C8-1, except that the launch-pack spec accepts Quillmoor's don't rules for its picked ads.
